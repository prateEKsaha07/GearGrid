# GearGrid — Submission Description

*Sector: Agriculture — "How can small farmers share expensive equipment instead of owning it?"*

**Problem & Solution:** Small and marginal farmers often cannot afford tractors, tillers, harvesters or sprayers, forcing them to either forgo mechanization or overpay informal middlemen for rentals. Meanwhile, equipment owned by larger farmers sits idle for most of the season with no reliable way to reach nearby farmers who need it. GearGrid is a peer-to-peer equipment-sharing marketplace where owners list idle machinery and farmers who need it can browse or post a request; owners then choose from incoming bids. Unlike a one-time goods sale, GearGrid uses a slot-based calendar so equipment can be booked by multiple farmers across different dates without conflict.

**Target Audience:** Equipment owners — mid-size farmers with idle tractors, tillers, harvesters or sprayers looking for extra income. Renters — small and marginal farmers who cannot afford to own equipment. Rural youth — potential owner-operators who can earn by offering equipment for hire.

**Social Issue & Impact:** Small farmers are priced out of mechanized farming, while informal rental markets are inconsistent and often exploitative. GearGrid lowers the cost barrier to mechanization, creates a new income stream for owners during idle periods, and strengthens local rural economies by keeping resource-sharing within the community instead of relying on middlemen.

**How it works:** Owners list equipment with available dates. Renters browse or post a need — including by voice, for low-literacy users. Multiple bids per listing are handled by an overlap-aware calendar, so non-conflicting bookings can coexist. At pickup and return, both parties complete a digital agreement, exchange an OTP, and upload condition photos — creating a trust and evidence layer that protects both sides. A security deposit (paid directly between the two parties and confirmed by both in the app) and reliability scoring deter fraud and no-shows. Every completed rental ends with an itemised invoice covering the original price, extension charges, deposit, platform commission and taxes. Renters can request extensions if more time is needed, subject to owner approval and calendar availability. Owners retain full control over relisting after each rental rather than the platform auto-relisting on their behalf.

**Reuse & Feasibility:** GearGrid adapts a proven, partially-built reverse-marketplace architecture (request/bid lifecycle, pincode matching, reliability scoring, auto-expiry) into the equipment-rental context, making it realistically buildable within the challenge timeline while introducing genuine new technical elements — slot scheduling, digital agreements, and fraud safeguards — suited to sharing physical, high-value assets.
